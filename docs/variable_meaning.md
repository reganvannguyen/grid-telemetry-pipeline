
Weather Metrics

• Dew Point Temperature: The temperature to which air must cool to reach 100% humidity and start forming dew, fog, or frost.
• Relative Humidity (RH): The percentage of water vapor currently in the air compared to the maximum amount it can hold at that temperature.
• Station Pressure (Stn Press): The raw atmospheric air pressure measured directly at a weather station's actual elevation without adjusting to sea level.
• Wind Chill: A "feels-like" index measuring how fast exposed skin loses heat due to combined cold temperatures and wind speed.

Power Grid Metrics (AESO)

• CSDGen (Current Supply & Demand Generation): Total gross electricity produced by large, utility-scale power plants with a capacity of 5 MW or greater.
• SysGen (System Generation): Net metered electricity actually delivered onto the public transmission grid by local power plants.
• BTFGen (CSDGen - SysGen): Electricity produced by large generators and consumed immediately on-site ("behind the fence") by industrial facilities.
• Load (System Load): Total metered electricity pulled off the public transmission grid by homes, businesses, and non-self-generating industry.
• ActLoad (BTFGen + Load): True total electricity consumption in a planning area (or province when summed), combining public grid Load and private industrial BTFGen.
